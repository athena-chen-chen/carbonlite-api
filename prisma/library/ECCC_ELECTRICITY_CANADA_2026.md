# Canada 2026 location-based Scope 2 electricity

CarbonLite v0.1 adopts the provincial and territorial electricity consumption
intensities in Environment and Climate Change Canada, *Emission factors and
reference values*, Version 4.0 (September 2026), Table 5.3, as its disclosed
corporate **Scope 2 — Location-based — Canada — calendar year 2026**
methodology.

ECCC publishes these values for Canada's federal offset/reference-value
framework and describes consumption intensity as the GHG intensity of
electricity delivered to the consumer. CarbonLite separately adopts those
grid-consumption-intensity values for corporate inventory accounting. This is
not a claim that ECCC labels Table 5.3 a corporate Scope 2 standard.

Each source value is divided by exactly 1,000 to convert gCO2e/kWh to
kgCO2e/kWh. Selection requires electricity, kWh, an exact province or territory,
and activity year 2026. There is no national, nearest-year, or prior-year
fallback. The legacy Alberta 0.53 factor, 2025 ECCC references, and Alberta EGDF
remain ineligible for 2026 ordinary corporate Scope 2.

The pack calculates location-based Scope 2 only. Market-based Scope 2 is not
calculated unless qualifying contractual data is provided; it is never reported
as zero. Existing persisted calculation snapshots are not rewritten.

Source: [ECCC Version 4.0, Table 5.3](https://www.canada.ca/en/environment-climate-change/services/climate-change/pricing-pollution-how-it-will-work/output-based-pricing-system/federal-greenhouse-gas-offset-system/emission-factors-reference-values.html).
